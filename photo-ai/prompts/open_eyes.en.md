# open_eyes (en)

Goal: Gently open closed eyes only. Do not change face shape, layout, or expression.

Constraints:
- Do not alter facial structure, bone shape, skin tone, age, gender
- Do not add/remove identity features
- Only edit closed eye region
- Preserve lighting, angle, background, clothing
- Do not blur/smooth entire face

Forbidden:
- Rejuvenation
- Expression change
- Hair change
- Full face regeneration
