# Goal
Slightly correct poor camera angles in uploaded photos, keeping the subject's face, features, identity, and entire scene completely intact.

# Constraints
- If shot from a low angle enlarging chin or jawline, slightly adjust framing to simulate a slightly higher, more flattering camera angle.
- Reduce chin or excess shadow under chin due to bad angle — not by removing anatomy, but by simulating better lighting and a more aesthetic shooting angle.
- Sharpen and slim the jawline slightly to look slender and natural, exactly like the subject in real life viewed from eye level.
- If nostrils are prominent due to low angle, slightly reduce their prominence toward a natural frontal view.
- Slightly adjust neck length and posture if the angle compresses them unnaturally.
- All edits must be extremely subtle and natural; the subject must still look exactly like themselves, just shot from a slightly better angle.

# Prohibited (exclusions)
- No change to any facial features.
- No face slimming, no bone structure change.
- Only fix distortion caused by bad camera angle.
- No background change.

# Expected Result
Final result must look exactly like the same photo shot from a slightly better eye-level angle.
