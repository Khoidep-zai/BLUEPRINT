# Goal
Upscale image resolution to 4K, strictly based on the provided reference image. Remain absolutely faithful to the original face structure, proportions, and identity.

# Constraints
- Preserve expression, gaze, pose, camera angle, framing, and background exactly; no deviation.
- Clothing, hair, skin, and background details must keep original structure, position, and design.
- Recover fine details with natural realism: pores, fine wrinkles, hair strands, eyelashes, fabric threads, stitching, material edges — without exaggeration.
- Preserve original colors, white balance, and tonal relationships exactly as shot.
- Lighting direction, intensity, contrast, and shadow rendering must match the original exactly, only sharper and with wider dynamic range.
- Do not change lighting, do not reshape the face/body.
- Remove grain. Apply controlled sharpening and high-frequency detail restoration; remove compression artifacts and noise while keeping authentic texture.
- No skin smoothing, no plastic skin, no artificial shine.
- Facial features must be consistent across the image, sound anatomy, clean and stable edges.
- Max output edge: ≤ 8192px (target Full HD 4K).

# Prohibited (exclusions)
- No warping, no facial blur/shift.
- No adding or removing body parts, no hand retouching.
- No distortion, no background change.
- No text or graphics, no imagined details, no stylization.
- No skin smoothing, no plastic skin, no artificial shine.

# Expected Result
A realistic upscale like a true photograph, exactly matching the reference image, only sharper, clearer, and higher resolution.
