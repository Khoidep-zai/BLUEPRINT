# Goal
In group photos, identify anyone with closed or half-closed eyes and open their eyes naturally — only for those people.

# Constraints
- Match eye shape, eyelid position, gaze direction, and face lighting for each person.
- Do not change face structure, expression, skin texture, hair style, head position, or camera angle.
- Do not borrow eyes from other people.
- Do not enlarge eye size or brightness.
- Result must look like a real photo shot at the same moment when everyone has eyes open.

# Prohibited (exclusions)
- No change to face structure, expression, skin texture, hair style, head position, camera angle.
- No borrowing eyes from others.
- No enlarging eye size or brightness.

# Expected Result
Eyes opened naturally for people with closed/half-closed eyes, looking like a real photo shot at the same moment.
