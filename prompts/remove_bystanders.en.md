# Goal
Remove all people in the background of the uploaded photo. Keep the main subject exactly intact.

# Constraints
- Do not change the main subject's face, body, clothing, pose, or expression.
- Recreate background naturally where people were removed, matching original location, architecture, lighting, shadows, and depth.
- No cropping, no blurring, no stylization.
- Result must look like a photo originally shot without people in the background.

# Prohibited (exclusions)
- No change to main subject (face, body, clothing, pose, expression).
- No cropping.
- No excessive blurring.
- No stylization.

# Expected Result
Image looks like it was originally shot without people in the background.
