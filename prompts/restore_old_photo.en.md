# Goal
Restore the photo using era-appropriate techniques, handling all time-related issues: blur, damage, fading, scratches, tears, creases, worn spots, or black-and-white photos.

# Constraints
- Analyze the image to determine the approximate era and original shooting process, ensuring historically accurate restoration.
- Make the image fresh and clear by lightly sharpening soft edges and facial features without overdoing it.
- Smooth grain or noise if present, and recreate missing parts with authentic texture matching the original.
- If color is faded or missing, restore color naturally and vividly but appropriate to the photography technology of that era, not looking fake.
- Balance color according to natural light, adjust brightness and contrast so everything stands out, and keep original tonal values.
- Add fine details for faces, objects, or background that may have been lost, such as fabric folds on clothing, living skin texture, or small details in the scene.
- Keep overall realism, preserve natural film grain and do not change layout.
- Ensure the whole image is balanced, no harsh shadows or blown-out highlights, remove technical flaws but still respect vintage character and exposure characteristics of that era.
- Upscale to higher resolution such as **Full HD 4K**, output in realistic style like a true photograph.

# Prohibited (exclusions)
- No layout change.
- No fake-looking colors inappropriate to the era.
- No harsh shadows or blown-out highlights.
- No change to vintage character and exposure characteristics of that era.

# Expected Result
Professionally restored photo or high-quality newly shot photograph, realistic, sharp, era-appropriate, Full HD 4K resolution.
